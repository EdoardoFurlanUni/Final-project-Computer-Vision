We designed our system under very strict conditions, such as a fixed camera, consistent lighting, and a controlled environment. However, in real-world applications these assumptions are rarely satisfied.\\
A key limitation of our project is its lack of robustness to scale variations and differences in image resolution. As a result, the system cannot generalize to diverse environments and remains applicable only under carefully controlled conditions.\\
Another significant limitation is that our model is not capable of processing video in real time, which represents a crucial requirement for many practical use cases.\\
We also encountered issues related to lighting. The flat surface used in our setup was illuminated by a lamp positioned close to it, causing uneven lighting across the image. This resulted in a strong brightness difference between the center and the borders of the frame, which negatively affected detection accuracy. In particular, coins placed directly under the lamp appeared overly bright, leading the model to frequently misclassify them or even fail in detecting them.
