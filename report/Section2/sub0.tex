
In this section, the performances of our algorithm are analyzed based on:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{mIoU}: computed for each image/frame as the intersection over union between detected and ground truth circles in a way that both false positives and false negatives are considered;
    \item \textbf{accuracy}: which indicates the ratio of correctly classified circles over the total number of ground truth circles;
    \item \textbf{total sum estimation};
    \item \textbf{computational time}.
\end{itemize}
